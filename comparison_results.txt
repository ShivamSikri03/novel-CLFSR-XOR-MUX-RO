4. KEY COMPARATIVE ADVANTAGES OF `mux_xor_clfsr`
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Ultra-Low Resource Utilization:
   Consumes only 71 LUTs (0.34%) and 112 Flip-Flops (0.27%) on Artix-7, compared to 320 LUTs/320 DFFs in [12] 
   and 298 LUTs in [13], leaving >99% of FPGA resources free for user cryptographic accelerators (AES, SHA-3, ECC).

2. High Throughput with Large Slack Margin:
   Operates at 100.00 Mbps under the onboard 100 MHz oscillator with a positive timing slack margin of +4.944 ns. 
   When clocked up to its maximum frequency (f_max = 197.79 MHz), the throughput doubles to 197.79 Mbps.

3. Low Power Footprint & High Efficiency:
   Total on-chip dissipation is just 95 mW (23 mW dynamic + 72 mW static), delivering an exceptional power 
   efficiency of 1.05 to 2.08 Mbps/mW.

4. Superior Statistical Quality:
   Unlike [12], [13], Logistic, or Tent map TRNGs that fail several NIST tests or show duty cycle bias, 
   mux_xor_clfsr passes 100% of NIST SP 800-22 tests (15/15) with an outstanding min-entropy of 0.9967 bits/symbol.
